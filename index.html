How to explain it

The arrow is the Bloch vector. |0⟩ is the north pole and |1⟩ is the south pole. X takes |0⟩ to |1⟩. Y also moves the Bloch vector to the |1⟩ position, but the actual state is i|1⟩ because its β amplitude is imaginary.
